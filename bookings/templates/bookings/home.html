How it works

Questions

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the size, age and colours of the tattoo, and your skin. Most tattoos take several sessions. We'll give you an estimate at your consultation.

Does it hurt?

Most people describe it as a sharp snapping feeling. Sessions are short, and we use cooling to keep you comfortable.

Why do I need a patch test?

It shows how your skin reacts to the laser before we treat a larger area. You need one before your first treatment, at least 48 hours before.
